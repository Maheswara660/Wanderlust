Explore Top Destinations

A curated collection of the world's most spectacular places, complete with itineraries and local guides.

Europe

Santorini, Greece

Aegean Sea, Europe

Santorini is famous for its dramatic sea views, blue-domed white houses, active volcanic caldera, and striking red sand beaches. Indulge in sunset strolls and crisp local white wines.

Asia

Kyoto, Japan

Kansai Region, Asia

Immerse yourself in Japan's cultural heartland. Walk through tranquil bamboo forests, explore thousands of vermilion Shinto gates, and visit spectacular classical gardens and wooden temples.

South America

Machu Picchu, Peru

Andes Mountains, Peru

Perched high in the Andes mountains, this mysterious 15th-century Inca citadel features mortar-free stone masonry, steep terraced fields, and panoramic views of pristine mountain ridges.

Europe

Swiss Alps, Switzerland

Central Alps, Europe

A wonderland for outdoor enthusiasts. Hike through emerald green valleys past wood chalets in the summer, or enjoy world-class skiing and cheese fondue next to peak views.
